🎯 HOW IT WORKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. SIGN UP
   Create account with email & password

2. ONBOARDING
   Tell us about yourself, salary, and expenses (3 easy steps)

3. ADD DEBTS
   List your credit cards, loans, and EMIs
   → System calculates when you'll be debt-free

4. TRACK EXPENSES
   Log your daily spending
   → App shows where your money goes

5. GET SAFE LIMIT
   Know exactly how much you can spend today
   → Based on remaining days and obligations

6. PLAN GOALS
   Create goals (marriage, home, education)
   → System tells you if realistic

7. CHECK COACH
   Get personalized weekly recommendations
   → Understand your spending patterns

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔑 KEY CALCULATIONS (Automatic)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SAFE-TO-SPEND
   Available Daily = (Income - Fixed Expenses - EMI - Goal Savings) / Days Left

DEBT-FREE DATE
   Using Avalanche method (highest interest first)
   Months to payoff = Outstanding / (EMI - Monthly Interest)

FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORE (0-100)
   • Emergency fund:    20 points
   • Debt-to-income:    25 points
   • Savings rate:      20 points
   • Net worth growth:  15 points
   • Goal progress:     10 points
   • Insurance:         10 points
